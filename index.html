The Problem

Manual SOS apps fail
when you need them most

Existing safety tools assume you can act. Real emergencies don't.

Can't press a button during an attack

Panic, restraint, or shock make manual triggers unreachable. Help shouldn't depend on your ability to tap a screen.

GPS drains battery in 2 hours

Continuous location tracking kills your phone before you reach home. A dead phone is a dead safety net.

False alarms desensitize guardians

When every stumble triggers an alert, families start ignoring them. Precision matters more than volume.

How It Works

Autonomous protection
in 4 steps

From a single tap to a sent alert — seamlessly, silently.

1
Start

One tap starts monitoring. Then pocket your phone. Nirbhay runs quietly in the background.

Accelerometer and gyroscope continuously read your motion — flagging irregular struggle patterns.

Technology

Built for
India's realities

Reliability > Precision

Designed for low-signal, low-battery, real-world India.

GRU Neural Network

94% threat detection accuracy · 200ms on-device inference

Runs entirely on your phone. No cloud required for detection — your data never leaves your device during analysis.

Cell Tower Triangulation

5% battery drain vs. 30% with GPS

We triangulate via cell towers instead of GPS. Your phone lasts all day, and your location is still accurate enough to matter.

SMS Fallback

Works without internet via Fast2SMS

Even in areas with no data signal, alerts reach your family via SMS. No app installation needed on the guardian's side.

Offline Queue

Zero alerts lost to connectivity gaps

All alerts are queued locally and fire the moment signal returns. Dead zones don't create safety gaps.
